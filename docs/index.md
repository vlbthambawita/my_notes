## How each lesson works

1. **TL;DR**: the whole idea in 2–3 sentences.
2. **Step by step**: one new idea per step, each with a visual.
3. **Try it**: interactive widgets where you change things and see what happens.
4. **Check yourself**: quick questions with hidden answers.
